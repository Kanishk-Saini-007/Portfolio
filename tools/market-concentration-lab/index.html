Market Concentration Lab

Compute concentration from a defined market, and show how a proposed combination changes a descriptive HHI measure.

Runs fully in your browser Synthetic example included Learning tool · not investment advice
What it does

Measures how concentrated a defined market is, using HHI and CR4, and shows how a proposed combination of two firms would change that descriptive measure.

What you enter

Each firm's share of the market you define, and which two firms propose to combine. Shares can be percentages or revenues. The prefilled example is synthetic.

How to read the results

HHI and CR4 before and after the combination, plus the effective number of equal-size firms. Higher means more concentrated.

Keep in mind

Everything depends on how you define the market. This is a descriptive measure, not a legal or regulatory test.

Inputs

JSON input, same schema as the command-line version's example.json. Edit values and run. Ctrl+Enter runs too. Your edits stay in this browser only; nothing is sent anywhere.

Same calculation as the Python CLI in the linked repo; identical input gives identical output.
