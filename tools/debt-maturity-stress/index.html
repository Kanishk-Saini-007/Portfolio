Debt Maturity Stress

Model yearly liquidity after scheduled debt maturities and operating cash flows, without assuming automatic refinancing.

Runs fully in your browser Synthetic example included Learning tool · not investment advice
What it does

Tests whether cash on hand plus operating cash flow covers each year's scheduled debt repayments, without assuming the company can refinance.

What you enter

Starting cash, expected operating cash flow per year, and the debt maturities due each year. The prefilled example is synthetic.

How to read the results

The first shortfall year is when the company can no longer pay from its own resources. Minimum cash shows the worst point across the years.

Keep in mind

No new borrowing, asset sales or rescues are assumed. This is a stress view, not a forecast.

JSON input, same schema as the command-line version's example.json. Edit values and run. Ctrl+Enter runs too. Your edits stay in this browser only; nothing is sent anywhere.
