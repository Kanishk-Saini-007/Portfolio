Earnings Quality Bridge

Reconcile reported profit with operating cash flow, working-capital movements and a clearly named residual.

Runs fully in your browser Synthetic example included Learning tool · not investment advice
What it does

Checks whether reported profit actually turns into cash, by walking from net income to operating cash flow.

What you enter

One period's figures, in any consistent unit: net income, operating cash flow, depreciation, the growth in receivables, inventory and payables, and capex. The prefilled example is synthetic.

How to read the results

Cash conversion near or above 1 means profit is backed by cash. A large unexplained residual means cash moved for reasons these inputs don't capture.

Keep in mind

A residual is a prompt to read the cash-flow statement, not proof of manipulation. Hard to interpret for loss-making companies.
