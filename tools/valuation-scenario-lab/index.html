Valuation Scenario Lab

Run explicit discounted-cash-flow scenarios and expose the share of modeled value coming from the terminal assumption.

Runs fully in your browser Synthetic example included Learning tool · not investment advice
What it does

Values a company under explicit scenarios using discounted cash flow, and shows how much of the answer rests on the long-term terminal assumption.

What you enter

Current free cash flow, then per scenario: growth years, growth rate, discount rate, terminal growth. Plus net debt and shares outstanding. The prefilled example is synthetic.

How to read the results

Value per share for each scenario. A high terminal share means most of the value sits in the distant-future assumption, not the forecast years.

Keep in mind

A DCF is an argument about assumptions, not a price target. Small input changes move the answer a lot.
